# Slide quality

- 16:9 wide; title normally 30–36 pt; body normally at least 18 pt.
- Use the central theme, masters, grid, margins and spacing helpers.
- Avoid bullet walls, ornamental cards, decorative icons and full-slide screenshots.
- Preserve whitespace and direct attention to the evidence supporting the title.
- Split content when a slide cannot remain legible without shrinking.
