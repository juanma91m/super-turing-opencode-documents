# Visual QA checklist

Check overflow, clipping, overlap, unsafe margins, tiny text, orphaned headings, illegible charts, inconsistent alignment, weak hierarchy, poor contrast, excessive density, awkward whitespace, repetition, empty pages/slides and detached captions.

For forms, also check that section spacing is stronger than heading-to-divider spacing; dividers remain visually attached to headings; handwriting lines are regular and appropriately dense; privacy notices are separated from writable areas; callouts have safe internal padding; repeated page furniture stays unobtrusive; and every requested format preserves field labels, row counts, page grouping and writing capacity without accidental duplicates.

Cross-format consistency means comparable hierarchy, density and semantics, not pixel-perfect equality between renderers.

Severity: `CRITICAL` blocks use or correctness; `MAJOR` materially harms comprehension or professionalism; `MINOR` is a localized polish issue.
